Privacy Policy: Ontario ALS/BLS

Effective date: June 9, 2026. Updated November 8, 2026.

Ontario ALS/BLS ("the app") is a quick-reference tool for Ontario paramedics. This policy explains how it handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect anything, and the only information the developer receives is an email you choose to send.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking. It does not contain advertising. After you install it, the app does not send any information about you or your use of it anywhere.

Data stored on your device

A small amount of information is saved on your own device, so the app remembers your preferences. The developer cannot see it:

  • Whether you have accepted the disclaimer.
  • Whether you have already seen the opening screen.
  • The directives you have saved (your "Saved" list).
  • Which sections of the contents list you have opened.

This information stays on your phone. If your phone's own backup is on (Android backup, or iCloud Backup or a computer backup on iPhone), it can be included in that backup and come back if you reinstall the app or restore the phone, so deleting the app does not always remove it. On Android you can also clear the app's storage in Settings.

Network use

The app is designed to work fully offline. The three documents are inside the app, so it does not download anything or make network requests while you use it. If you tap a link under "Official sources" or "Privacy", that page opens in your web browser, outside the app.

Feedback you choose to send

If you tap "Send app feedback," the app opens your own email application with a message addressed to the developer. Nothing is sent automatically and nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. If you choose to send a bug report, the message includes basic technical details (your phone model, its operating system and web view versions, the app version, and your screen size) to help fix the problem. You can see and edit that information before sending, and no name, email address, or location is added by the app. General feedback (an idea or comment) includes no device information at all.

Messages you send to OntarioPCSsupport@gmail.com, and the email address they come from, are read by the developer and kept so a problem can be answered and fixed. To have your messages deleted, ask at the same address.

Children

The app is intended for paramedics. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: OntarioPCSsupport@gmail.com
