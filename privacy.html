Privacy Policy: Ontario ALS/BLS

Effective date: June 9, 2026. Updated September 21, 2026.

Ontario ALS/BLS ("the app") is a quick-reference tool for Ontario paramedics. This policy explains how it handles your information. The short version: it does not collect anything.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking. It does not contain advertising. After you install it, the app does not send any information about you or your use of it anywhere.

Data stored on your device

A small amount of information is saved only on your own device, so the app remembers your preferences. This never leaves your phone and is not visible to the developer or anyone else:

  • Whether you have accepted the disclaimer.
  • Whether you have already seen the opening screen.
  • The directives you have saved (your "Saved" list).
  • Which sections of the contents list you have opened.

Deleting the app erases all of it. On Android you can also clear the app's storage in Settings.

Network use

The app is designed to work fully offline. The three standards are bundled inside the app, so it does not download anything or make network requests while you use it. If you tap a link under "Official sources" or "Privacy", that page opens in your web browser, outside the app.

Feedback you choose to send

If you tap "Send app feedback," the app opens your own email application with a message addressed to the developer. Nothing is sent automatically and nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. If you choose to send a bug report, the message includes basic technical details (your phone model, its operating system and web view versions, the app version, and your screen size) to help fix the problem. You can see and edit that information before sending, and no name, email address, or location is added by the app. General feedback (an idea or comment) includes no device information at all.

Children

The app is intended for paramedics. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location with a new effective date.
