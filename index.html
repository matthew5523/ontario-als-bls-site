Ontario ALS/BLS

A free, offline reference app for Ontario paramedics. It searches and displays the Ontario Basic Life Support Patient Care Standards (v3.4), the Advanced Life Support Patient Care Standards (v5.4) and the ALS PCS Companion Document (version 5.4, August 2026) exactly as published, page for page.

How to get it

iPhone. Ontario ALS/BLS is on its way to the App Store in Canada. It needs iOS 17.4 or later. Until it appears there, you can install it through Apple's TestFlight:

  1. Open testflight.apple.com/join/jQZYAkzY on the device.
  2. Tap View in TestFlight. If TestFlight is not installed, install it from the App Store, then open the link again.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install.

Android. The Android version is in a closed test, so it cannot be installed from a public link yet. Email the Gmail address on your phone to OntarioPCSsupport@gmail.com and you will be added to the test and sent your own install link.
