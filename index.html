Ontario ALS/BLS

A free, offline reference and study app for Ontario paramedics and paramedic students. It searches and displays the Ontario Basic Life Support Patient Care Standards (v3.4), the Advanced Life Support Patient Care Standards (v5.4) and the ALS PCS Companion Document exactly as published, page for page.
